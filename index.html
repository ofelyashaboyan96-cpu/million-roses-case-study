01 · Overview

A boutique feeling, online.

Million Roses is a flower boutique concept for Yerevan. The goal was a store that feels as calm and premium as a real florist's studio, while making ordering a same-day bouquet fast on any phone.

02 · The challenge

Flowers are bought with emotion,
and in a hurry.

i

Trust at first glance

Buyers can't smell or touch the bouquet, so photography, calm colours and honest details must do the work.

ii

Speed on mobile

Most gifts are ordered on a phone, often the same day. The path from home page to checkout had to be short and clear.

iii

Three languages

Customers in Armenia read Armenian, Russian or English, so every custom section had to translate cleanly.

03 · Design system

Four colours, one mood.

A palette taken from the flowers themselves: deep stem green for actions, warm cream for calm, sage for softness and gold reserved for prices and small details.

04 · What I built

Every section has a job.

Custom section

Our collections

A curated product showcase with tabs for baskets, bouquets and plants. Visitors switch between categories instantly, without leaving the home page, and see prices, sale badges and bestsellers at a glance.

  • Tabs built in Liquid + JavaScript
  • Sale and bestseller badges
  • Swipeable on phones
Conversion

Same-day delivery banner

A full-width video banner with a live countdown to the daily 16:00 order cutoff (Yerevan time). It answers the customer's biggest question, "will it arrive today?", and adds gentle urgency. A running strip below repeats the key promises.

  • Separate desktop & mobile videos
  • JavaScript countdown timer
  • Text in three languages
Product page

A calm, clear product page

Thumbnail gallery, a clear green call to action and a short delivery note under the price. "What's inside", "Size" and "Care" open as tidy rows filled from product metafields, followed by a product FAQ.

  • Content from metafields, not hardcoded
  • Same-day cutoff note in the buy box
  • FAQ with structured data for Google
Marketing

Welcome popup

A calm sign-up popup offering 10% off the first order. It grows the email list without blocking the shopping experience and saves subscribers straight into Shopify customers.

  • Shows once, respects the visitor
  • Discount code delivered on sign-up
Content

Flower care & blog

Four simple care tips on the home page lead to a full care guide on the blog. Useful content builds trust, keeps customers happy after delivery and helps the store appear in search.

  • Editable tips in the theme editor
  • Custom blog post layout
