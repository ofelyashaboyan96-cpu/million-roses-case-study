The story behind the name

Why Million Roses?

Because one rose can say something. A million can tell an entire story.

Million Roses was born from a simple belief: flowers are more than something beautiful to look at. They carry feelings, memories and moments that deserve to be remembered.

Every bloom we grow, choose and arrange is part of that idea. From our hands to yours, flowers made not simply to be seen, but to make someone feel something.
