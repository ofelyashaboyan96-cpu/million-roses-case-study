What I built

Four sections. Four jobs.

01 · Sales

Our collections

Baskets, bouquets and plants in one place, switched with tabs. Customers compare without leaving the home page, and sale and bestseller badges guide the choice.

02 · Conversion

Same-day delivery

A live countdown to the 16:00 cutoff answers the gift buyer's first question, "will it arrive today?", and turns a visit into an order.

03 · Trust

The product page

One clear call to action, a delivery note in the buy box, and "What's inside", "Size" and "Care" rows filled from metafields. Every doubt is answered before checkout.

04 · Marketing

Welcome popup

10% off the first order in exchange for an email. It grows a list the shop owns, for launches, holidays and repeat orders.
